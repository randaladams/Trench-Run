## Full description (max 4000)
Steer by tilting your phone and race a fighter down a long, narrow trench at full speed.

Bridges, raised floors, hanging blocks, pillars and walls come at you faster the farther you get. Hold the left side of the screen to fire your lasers, and hold the right side to pull in power-ups with your tractor beam.

HOW IT PLAYS
- Tilt left and right to steer, forward and back to dive and climb
- Hold the left side to fire lasers at drones and wall turrets
- Hold the right side for the tractor beam: drag power-up orbs toward you
- Your shield takes hits for you, but every crash, laser and wall scrape drains it

POWER-UPS
- Cyan orbs recharge your shield
- Green orbs upgrade your lasers, up to triple shot
- Violet hourglass orbs slow the whole trench down

WATCH OUT FOR
- Wall turrets that glow, whine, and then fire right where you are
- Laser heat: fire in bursts or your guns overheat
- Scraping the floor or walls: sparks fly and your shield drains

ALSO
- Guided tutorial where you can't crash
- Scoreboard with your best run and your last 19 runs
- 10 badges to earn
- One continue per run
- Sounds and spoken shield warnings, pitch controls you can flip
- Plays offline. No account, no sign-in.

Free to play with ads.
